Etienne Le Naour

I work on foundation models for time series: pretraining models that learn general-purpose representations of temporal data, and making them adapt to new series and tasks at inference time, through in-context learning rather than retraining. My research spans representation learning, continuous-time models and their use for forecasting, imputation, classification and other downstream tasks.
